## Item Repair Hub v2.9.0

By DaggerHashimoto. Renewal and pre-renewal.

### 23 more iRO item repairs (55 in all, renewal only)

On by default, behind the same **iRO effect repairs** switch. They follow the official iRO item descriptions; set and Grade bonuses are not included.

**Missing effects added**
- **Gate of Netherworld:** its +7 and +9 bonuses never applied at any refine, because the official script checks an unset variable. They work now.
- **Bear Aimed Bag, Melody Wing:** Perfect Hit and SP cost effects, and +12/+13 damage and no size penalty.
- **Screaming Ghost Party:** +12 and +13 bonuses.
- **Orc Ancestor Armor:** size damage now scales with First Faith Power at +12, plus +13 Rampage Blast damage.
- **Giant Shield:** +9 and +11 effects.
- **Archangel Aegis:** +12 Tiger Cannon.
- **Ignis Flame Guard:** +9 cooldowns, the HP and SP cost of its +11 proc, and +12 skill damage.
- **Ancient Royal Guard Greaves:** per-refine and +10/+11 bonuses. Genesis Ray and Earth Drive damage now needs +13.
- **Constellation Blessing:** all stats grow per refine, plus +11 and +12 bonuses.
- **Coatneis Knuckle:** indestructible, per-refine skill damage, and +13 and +15 bonuses.

**Now use iRO's refine levels or effects, so they are weaker at lower refines**
Kuraokami's Feather Robe, Fairy of Eden, Authority Sandals, Stylish Silent Shoes, Legacy of Wise One, Ghost Whisper, Awaking Bloom, Twinhead Dragon Mail, Four of a Kind, Loud Park, Spenta Armaiti, Gracies Aranea. Giant Shield's critical damage and Ancient Royal Guard Greaves' skill damage also move to higher refines.

To turn off one item, add it under Add items in the editor, choose **Remove item**, then Save & Export.

### Support the author

If you enjoy the mod, there is an optional ☕ **Support the author** button at the top right of the editor, for buying DaggerHashimoto a coffee. The app does not open new windows, so it copies the link for you. The × hides it until the next version.

### Updating

- **Find Mods:** update in the app, then Apply & restart.
- Your saved collections, live changes and presets are kept. If you made your own repairs, open Item Repairs after updating and Save & Export again, keeping the installed folder's `.source.json` when you replace it.
- A live change on one of these items keeps the script it was applied with until you apply it again or Undo it.

### Limits

These repairs were checked against the iRO descriptions and by automated tests; the new tiers have not each been played in game. Please report anything odd with **Copy report** in Live changes.
