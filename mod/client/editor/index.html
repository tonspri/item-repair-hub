What's new in v2.12.0
  • Save & apply live: the new button beside Save & Export saves your item changes and puts them in game at once: skills, autocasts, stats and the equip level, kept after restarts. Save & Export is now only needed for class families, transfer rules and the falcon, or to share the mod.
  • v2.11.0: new look: the same style as Item Collection UI, with a side menu (Add items, Included repairs, Live changes, What's new) and one panel.
  • Sizes: the gear at the top right sets Window, Text and Pictures (Small to Largest). Your choice is kept.
  • Repair check: Live changes now says which worn items IRH repairs, which have your edit, and lights up the refine tiers you have reached.
  • Support the author moved to the bottom, beside Save & Export. Mod Settings → Support the author button hides it.
  • v2.10.0: 21 more iRO item repairs (76 in all, renewal), on by default, including iRO's refine levels for Prana Boots, Pursuiter Shoes, Demon Hunting Shoes and Cylinder Boots. See Included repairs, iRO effect repairs.
  • New launcher: the IRH badge left of the RODEX icon, shown only once you are in game. Drag it anywhere; Escape menu → Reset Item Repairs icon puts it back. Escape menu → Item Repairs also opens this window.
  • v2.9.0: 23 more iRO item repairs (55 in all, renewal), on by default. Gate of Netherworld's +7 and +9 bonuses work at last. See Included repairs, iRO effect repairs.
  • Fix (v2.9.1): Save & Export from pre-renewal no longer leaves out a renewal collection saved by an older version.
  • Fix: custom skills on items with no script of their own (Main Gauche and many more) no longer stop Save & Export with "Missing baseline script".
  • Pre-renewal support: the editor and Live changes now work in pre-renewal too, with pre-renewal items and skills. Each era keeps its own collection; one Save & Export carries both.
  • The built-in repairs (iRO effects, Agni Sword, Cocytus Armor, All-in-One Buff Potion) stay renewal-only: they are renewal items.
  • In pre-renewal, the stat picker leaves out renewal-only trait stats (POW, P.ATK and so on).
