## Item Repair Hub v2.9.1

By DaggerHashimoto. Renewal and pre-renewal. A fix for v2.8.0 through v2.9.0.

### Fix

- **Save & Export keeps older renewal collections.** A renewal collection saved by a version before v2.8.0 was left out of any Save & Export made from pre-renewal, so the ZIP's renewal side came out empty. The collection itself was never lost: it stayed in your saved draft.

### Updating

- **Find Mods:** update in the app, then Apply & restart.
- If you exported from pre-renewal and your renewal repairs went missing, open Item Repairs after updating, Save & Export again from either era, and install that ZIP. Keep the installed folder's `.source.json` when you replace it.

Everything else is as in [v2.9.0](https://github.com/tonspri/item-repair-hub/releases/tag/v2.9.0), including the 55 iRO repairs.
